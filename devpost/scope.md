# AI Notes Maker

One line: AI-powered notes maker that turns multiple student-provided sources into organized, notebook-style study notes with a Coverage Check for important concepts.

## The Unique Kernel
A Coverage Check that extracts the important concepts from the provided sources and compares them to the generated notes, flagging gaps so students can review missed or under-covered topics.

## Who It's For
College and university students (exam preparation and course study) who gather information from lectures, textbooks, PDFs, and web pages and need a concise, organized study reference instead of scattered notes.

## The Core Loop
1. Student enters a topic and supplies a small set of sources (URLs or pasted text).
2. The system extracts important concepts and generates structured study notes with headings, definitions, explanations, examples, and source attributions.
3. Student runs the Coverage Check to see which important concepts from the sources are covered and which are missing.
4. Student reviews the notes and coverage feedback.

## What "Working" Looks Like
A one-minute demo: enter a topic such as "Photosynthesis", add two or three sources, click "Generate Notes", watch a notebook-style page populate with organized sections and source references, open the Coverage Check to show identified concepts and coverage status, then highlight a small section.
